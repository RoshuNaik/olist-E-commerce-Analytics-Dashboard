## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Power BI dashboard** built to analyze the sales and operational performance of the **Olist E-Commerce platform**.

The dashboard provides insights into **revenue, orders, customers, products, regions, delivery performance, and customer reviews**. Interactive filters allow users to explore business performance across different years and customer states.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

* Analyze overall e-commerce revenue and order performance
* Identify top-performing product categories
* Analyze revenue distribution across Brazilian states
* Evaluate delivery performance and late orders
* Understand customer review performance
* Identify regions with high delivery delays
* Provide an interactive dashboard for business decision-making

---

## 📑 Dashboard Pages

### 🏠 1. Landing Page

The landing page provides an overview of the business performance and navigation to different analytical sections.

It highlights key findings such as total revenue, orders, product revenue, regional concentration, and delivery performance.

### 📊 2. Executive Dashboard

Provides a high-level overview of the business through key performance indicators and visualizations.

**Key KPIs:**

* Total Revenue: **$16.01M**
* Total Orders: **96K**
* Total Customers: **99K**
* Average Delivery Days: **12.5 Days**
* Average Review Score: **4.09 / 5**

### 📦 3. Product Analysis

Analyzes product-level performance, including:

* Product category revenue
* Total product orders
* Total products sold
* Average item price
* Average freight value
* Average price vs. freight analysis

**Product Revenue:** **$13.6M**

### 🌎 4. Region Analysis

Analyzes revenue and order performance across Brazilian states.

The dashboard includes:

* Revenue by region
* Total regional orders
* Average delivery days
* Average freight value
* Geographic map visualization
* Top-performing regions

**Top Revenue-Generating Region:** São Paulo – approximately **$5.8M**

### 🚚 5. Delivery Analysis

Focuses on delivery performance and customer experience.

The dashboard analyzes:

* Average delivery days
* Revenue from delivered orders
* Delivered orders
* Late orders
* Delivery status
* Average delivery days by state

Approximately **93% of orders were delivered on time/early**, while around **7K orders were late**.

### 💰 6. Revenue Decomposition

The Revenue Decomposition page provides a deeper breakdown of revenue across:

* Product categories
* Seller states
* Order years

This helps identify how different dimensions contribute to overall revenue.

---

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

* The business generated approximately **$16.01M in total revenue** from **96K orders** and **99K customers**.
* Product revenue contributed approximately **$13.6M**.
* Revenue is highly concentrated in a few regions, with **São Paulo** being the strongest revenue-generating state.
* **Beleza Saúde** and **Relogios Presentes** are among the top-performing product categories.
* Overall delivery performance is strong, with approximately **93% of orders delivered on time or early**.
* **Roraima, Amapá, and Amazonas** have some of the highest average delivery times.
* Regional and delivery analysis can help identify opportunities to improve customer experience and reduce delays.
